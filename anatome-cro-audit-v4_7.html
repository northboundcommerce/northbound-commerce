Overview

Executive Summary

anatomē has all the components of a high-performing premium wellness DTC — a compelling founder story, a proprietary aromachology framework, strong product craft, and a loyal customer base. The site is leaving meaningful revenue on the table. Social proof is sparse to absent across the catalogue; the subscription mechanic is buried and over-hedged with commitment language; the navigation organises by product type rather than by need; and the returns experience is actively generating negative public reviews. This audit identifies ten highest-leverage opportunities and maps them to a phased 90-day roadmap. The brand is strong. The work is in aligning the commerce experience with that promise.

Findings

Top 10 opportunities

#OpportunityObservation & Recommendation AreaImpactEffort
01
Social proof absent on most products
Third-party data shows "No reviews" across a significant portion of the catalogue. For a wellness brand where efficacy drives purchase, absence of reviews is absence of permission to buy. Implement a post-purchase review flow (Okendo), surface stars on collection tiles, and pin review snippets above the fold on PDPs.PDP · CROHighLow
02
Subscription framed as commitment
The 20% subscriber saving is one of the site's strongest conversion mechanics — but treated as secondary to one-time purchase. "Minimum commitment of 2 months" introduces friction before a decision is made. Lead with the saving; soften the caveat. Test subscription as the default-selected option on high-velocity SKUs.PDP · CROHighLow
03
Navigation by product type, not need
A shopper arriving to "sleep better" navigates through product-type categories rather than a unified Sleep collection. Restructure primary nav around wellbeing pillars: Sleep, Focus, Calm, Movement, Skin — with product types as secondary filters within each.Nav · UXHighMedium
04
Returns policy damaging brand trust
Customers paying £4 to return an £8.50 product. Trustpilot reviews cite this specifically. The cost of one returned item is far lower than the cost of a publicly negative review that deters dozens of future buyers.CX · TrustHighLow
05
Homepage hero misses the brand USP
The meta title "Premium Wellbeing Products for Mind & Body in UK" is SEO boilerplate. The aromachology framework is the brand's real differentiator. The founder's story and scent-science positioning need to be the first thing a new visitor encounters.ATF · BrandMediumLow
06
No BNPL option at product price level
At £34–£210, the absence of Klarna is a missed opportunity, particularly for gift sets. Competitors in this price band routinely see basket size increases when BNPL is visible at the product price point. The Shopify app takes minutes to install.PDP · CROMediumLow
07
Workshops under-merchandised
Breathwork workshops, scent consultations, and practitioner collaborations build loyalty and justify premium pricing. Currently visible only at the bottom of the homepage. Surface in primary nav and as PDP cross-sells.CX · NavMediumLow
08
No loyalty mechanic beyond subscription
Wellness is inherently repeat-purchase. A loyalty programme (Smile.io) would increase purchase frequency, incentivise review-leaving, and give customers a reason to create accounts. A referral mechanic would drive new acquisition from the existing base.RetentionMediumMedium
09
Email cadence generating spam complaints
Trustpilot reviews cite daily emails with a non-functional unsubscribe path. High spam report rates degrade deliverability on all sends. A daily cadence is incompatible with a premium wellness brand whose core message is balance and calm.CRM · BrandMediumLow
10
International experience not calibrated
Ships to 30+ countries but the international experience is an afterthought: premium express air rates universally applied, 21-day delivery windows, UK-first copy throughout. Clearer delivery expectations and localised currency would reduce international drop-off.UX · GlobalMediumMedium

Section 01

Above the Fold & Navigation

ATF

Homepage — missed brand story

IssueHigh

Hero leads with category, not brand

New visitors from discovery channels have no compelling reason to believe anatomē is different from a generic wellness retailer. The aromachology framework and founder origin are the brand's actual moat.

✦ Lead with the brand's "why" — the transformative power of scent. Test an editorial hero with the philosophy statement and a single entry point ("Find your blend").

Free shipping threshold not visible ATF

Free shipping over £50 should be in the announcement bar on every page. A two-minute copy change with measurable AOV uplift.

Section 03

Customer Experience

IssueHigh

Returns policy incongruent with premium brand promise

Customers paying £4 to return an £8.50 product. Each Trustpilot review citing this is live marketing against the brand to every prospective buyer who searches "anatome reviews."

✦ Free returns on orders over £40. Self-service returns portal. Respond to all outstanding Trustpilot reviews with a public resolution.
IssueMedium

Email cadence damaging deliverability and brand trust

Daily send cadence and non-functional unsubscribe path have generated public complaints. A daily email barrage is a deep contradiction for a brand whose positioning is centred on calm and balance.

✦ Preference centre ("weekly / monthly / offers only"). Maximum 3x/week. One-click unsubscribe, functional within 10 days.

Section 04

Retention & Loyalty

IssueMedium

No loyalty programme for a repeat-purchase category

Wellness is inherently repeat-purchase. A loyalty programme extends the subscription mechanic to non-subscribers, incentivises reviews, and gives customers a reason to create accounts.

✦ Smile.io or Yotpo Loyalty. Points on purchases, reviews, and referrals. Launch with a founding-member double-points window.
IssueMedium

Workshops are a buried brand asset

Breathwork workshops, scent consultations, and practitioner collaborations build loyalty, justify premium pricing, and create shareable moments. Currently visible only at the bottom of the homepage.

✦ Dedicated Experiences page. "Experiences" in primary nav. PDP cross-sell: "Pair your sleep ritual with our Evening Workshop."

Implementation

Quick wins — 30 days

  • Install a reviews platform and launch post-purchase email flowTarget top 20 SKUs. Okendo integrates directly with Shopify and Klaviyo. Even 10 reviews per product moves conversion visibly.
  • Rewrite subscription copy across all PDPsReplace "minimum commitment of 2 months" with "cancel after your second order." Copy change only — no dev time required.
  • Add Klarna on-site messaging to PDPsAt £34–£210, the per-payment breakdown meaningfully lowers the purchase barrier — especially for gift sets.
  • Add free shipping threshold to announcement barSingle change with consistent AOV lift. Cart-drawer progress bar: "£X away from free delivery."
  • Audit email cadence and fix unsubscribe pathSpam complaints are visible on Trustpilot to every prospective buyer. Max 3x/week. Functional one-click unsubscribe.
  • Claim Trustpilot and respond to negative reviewsPublic responses with resolutions directly mitigate trust damage for every future visitor who reads them.
