Application Guide

The art of applying it right

01
Start with clean, toned skin
Apply after your Qasil Cleanser and Black Seed Toner have fully absorbed. The moisturiser seals the active work of both previous steps.
02
Take less than you think
A pea-sized amount is sufficient for face and neck. The formula is concentrated — overuse slows absorption and reduces efficacy.
03
Press, don't rub
Use fingertips to press gently into skin using upward, outward motions. Pressing — not rubbing — improves absorption and protects the skin barrier.
04
Morning and evening, consistently
Full HyperPrevent™ results build over 8–12 weeks. Consistency — especially at night — is when the formula does its deepest work.
