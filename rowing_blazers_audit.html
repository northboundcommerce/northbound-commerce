Executive Summary

Rowing Blazers has built something genuinely rare — a brand with real cultural cachet, a cult following, and a product range compelling enough to sustain 700% revenue growth between 2018 and 2022. The Shopify Plus store reflects that energy in its visual design. But beneath the editorial surface there are meaningful revenue leaks: a PDP that undersells its own product story, sizing confidence gaps fuelling real return friction, trust signals that disappear at exactly the wrong moments, and a checkout funnel that doesn't work as hard as it could. This audit identifies ten high-leverage opportunities — several addressable within 30 days with no significant development resource.

1
Above The Fold

Homepage & Entry Experience

Hero real estate underused for conversion intent

Impact: Med Effort: Low

The homepage leads strongly on editorial imagery but the primary CTA resolves to a broad collection rather than the current hero narrative. The collaboration engine — Paddington, Winnie-the-Pooh, Babar — is a genuine differentiator but competes equally with core product in nav, diluting both.

Recommendation: Ensure the ATF hero carries a single, seasonally-relevant CTA tied to the editorial story. Reserve the collaboration nav cluster for a clearly signposted sub-section so it reads as an asset, not noise.

Free shipping threshold ($195) not visible at entry

Impact: High Effort: Low

The free shipping threshold only surfaces on the locations page — not in the announcement bar or ATF. At this price point, $195 is often one item away for many visitors. Surfacing it early nudges shoppers toward add-to-bag rather than immediate checkout with a single item.

Recommendation: Add "Free US shipping on orders over $195" to the announcement bar. One line of copy, immediate AOV impact.

Welcome offer (20% off) not optimised for capture

Impact: Med Effort: Low

A 20% first-order discount is available for email sign-up — a strong incentive. If the Yieldify integration is firing on page-load rather than exit-intent or scroll-depth, it's training visitors to dismiss before they've invested in the experience.

Recommendation: Audit Yieldify trigger rules. Test exit-intent vs. time-on-page (45+ seconds). Reinforce the offer in the footer capture for those who dismissed the overlay.
2
Conversion Rate

Funnel & Cart Optimisation

Sizing confidence is the primary conversion blocker

Impact: High Effort: Med

Customer reviews across Trustpilot consistently cite sizing inconsistencies — mediums running large, model callouts not reflecting real proportions. This is a pre-purchase hesitation suppressing add-to-cart. The Barrel-built PDP includes size and fit detail, but not at the level of granularity needed to build real confidence at the point of decision.

Recommendation: Add a fit confidence module to every PDP: model height/weight/size worn, plus a per-garment fit note ("runs large in the body, true to sleeve"). Layer in a lightweight size recommendation quiz. Single highest-leverage CRO intervention available on this site.

Rebuy likely under-configured in cart

Impact: High Effort: Med

Rebuy is installed — one of the highest-ROI apps in the Shopify ecosystem when used well. Given the brand's strong product narrative (blazer, rugby, collaboration piece) there are natural "complete the look" moments that well-configured Rebuy rules could capitalise on in the cart drawer.

Recommendation: Audit Rebuy configuration. Set curated "pairs well with" rules per product type. In-cart upsells at the AOV threshold ("Add $X for free shipping") are a reliable, low-effort quick win.

No scarcity signalling on collaboration PDPs

Impact: High Effort: Low

For a brand that trades on limited-edition drops, there is no visible low-stock signal on product pages. The collaboration pieces — Paddington, Moomin, Babar, Magic 8 Ball, UNO — carry genuine scarcity logic that isn't being converted into urgency copy at the point of purchase.

Recommendation: Implement inventory-aware low-stock messaging ("Only 3 left in your size") on collaboration and seasonal PDPs. Native Shopify Liquid — minimal development required.
3
Brand & Trust

Reputation & Authority Signals

Customer service perception is a conversion suppressor

Impact: High Effort: Low

The volume and consistency of negative Trustpilot reviews — fulfilment delays, unresponsive CS, incorrect shipments — is notable for a brand at this price point. New visitors who Google the brand before purchasing will find these. The reputational fallout directly impacts conversion for cold traffic.

Recommendation: Prioritise Trustpilot review management — respond publicly to all reviews. A visible returns and CS policy on-site (ideally with a self-serve returns portal) reduces both contact volume and pre-purchase anxiety.

Broken page: MagicPass remnant on /pages/about-team

Impact: Med Effort: Low

The team page is displaying a broken MagicPass gate — an app recently uninstalled but whose Liquid code was not cleaned up. The page shows an error referencing a third-party app and a placeholder email address (name@university.com). Live now, visible to anyone navigating to learn about the brand.

Recommendation: Immediate fix. Remove the MagicPass Liquid snippet from the about-team page template. A 15-minute development task with real trust signal implications.
4
UX / UI

Navigation & Product Discovery

Mega-nav complexity may impede first-time discovery

Impact: Med Effort: Med

Seven top-level sections, each with multi-level sub-menus, serves a returning fan well. For a first-time visitor arriving from press or Instagram, it's cognitively heavy. The Edits section (Best Sellers, New Arrivals, Gifts Under $100) partially addresses this — but it's buried mid-nav rather than treated as a first-time visitor entry point.

Recommendation: Elevate "Best Sellers" and "New Arrivals" in the nav hierarchy. Consider a homepage module positioned explicitly for new visitors — a curated, non-intimidating introduction to the range.

Shopify Markets auto-geolocation may not trigger on first visit

Impact: Med Effort: Low

The Shopify Markets integration delivers currency localisation — critical for UK customers paying £180+ for a sweater. If the currency selector requires manual navigation rather than auto-triggering from geolocation, UK visitors are pricing in USD and potentially abandoning before they understand they're seeing the wrong number.

Recommendation: Confirm Shopify Markets is configured to auto-redirect based on geolocation on first visit. Verify the selector is also accessible below the fold and not exclusively within the navigation on mobile.
5
Content & Merchandising

Product Storytelling

Collaboration narrative stops at collection level — not carried to PDP

Impact: Med Effort: Med

The Barrel build introduced storytelling at collection landing page level, which is right. But the conversion opportunity lies in carrying that narrative into the PDP: why this character, why this garment, what makes this piece collectible. The PDP is where purchase decisions are made.

Recommendation: For all active collaboration PDPs, add a "The Story" module — 3–5 sentences on the collaboration origin and what makes this piece worth owning. Elevates the product from apparel to cultural artefact.
6
SEO

Search Visibility

Product structured data + AggregateRating will drive SERP rich results

Impact: Med Effort: Med

With a strong product catalogue and existing review presence, Rowing Blazers is well-positioned to benefit from Product schema with AggregateRating markup. Rich results showing star ratings in Google Search meaningfully improve organic CTR — particularly for branded product queries where competitor PDPs appear alongside.

Recommendation: Audit structured data implementation on PDPs. Ensure Product, Offer, and AggregateRating schema are correctly implemented and validated in Google's Rich Results Test.

Strategic Experiments
🧪

A/B Test: Fit Quiz vs. Static Size Guide on Blazers PDP

A lightweight fit recommendation widget on the blazer PDP, tested against the current static size chart. Hypothesis: reducing sizing anxiety at the point of decision increases add-to-cart rate and reduces returns.

Primary metric: ATC rate. Secondary: return rate by size.
📖

Collaboration Landing Pages: Story-first vs. Grid-first

Test a narrative-led collection page (editorial intro, then products) against the current product-grid-first layout for a single active collaboration. Hypothesis: story context increases session depth and conversion for first-time visitors arriving via social or press.

Primary metric: Collection-to-PDP click-through rate.
🛒

Cart Drawer: AOV Threshold Messaging ("You're $X away from free shipping")

Dynamic in-cart prompt when the threshold is not yet met, via Rebuy or a custom Liquid snippet. One of the highest-confidence experiments in premium apparel CRO — consistent lifts across comparable brands.

Primary metric: Average order value. Secondary: items per order.
