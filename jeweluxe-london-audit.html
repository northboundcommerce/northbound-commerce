Executive summary

The hard part is done

Jeweluxe is winning the most expensive thing to win: attention. An active, well-followed presence across Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest is generating real demand and sending qualified, high-intent visitors to the store. That is the foundation most jewellery brands spend years trying to build.

The commercial opportunity now sits entirely on-site. The pieces are beautifully made and properly credentialled — IGI-certified, UK-hallmarked, handcrafted in London — yet the buying experience doesn't carry those credentials to the moment of decision. The product page, where the sale is won or lost, omits the certification detail, sizing confidence, social proof and payment flexibility that a considered four-figure purchase requires. The same traffic, met with the buying experience of an established luxury house, converts at a materially higher rate and basket value.

The benchmark throughout is luxury, not mass-market. The recommendations favour certification transparency, private appointments and provenance storytelling over discounting and urgency tactics — the codes that brands like Boodles and De Beers use to justify the price and earn the trust.

Where the revenue is

Four moves, in priority order

Each converts demand you already have, rather than chasing more of it.

i.

Mirror the demand on-site

The audience and the happy customers exist; the proof isn't on the page. Activating reviews, customer imagery and press turns social momentum into on-site confidence exactly where buyers hesitate. The review tool is already installed — it simply isn't populated or surfaced.

ii.

Make bespoke a booked experience

Bespoke is the highest-value, highest-margin offer and currently routes high-intent enquiries into a general contact form. A bookable private appointment and a guided enquiry lift the conversation from a £1,350 catalogue ring to a £3,000+ commission.

iii.

Remove the price barrier

There is no way to spread the cost on pieces between £1,200 and £3,400. Interest-free instalments make four-figure purchases accessible to more of your existing traffic — without ever discounting the brand.

iv.

Tell the certification story

Every stone has an IGI report and a UK hallmark; neither appears on the product. Surfacing the full grade and provenance — the De Beers and Boodles standard — is what converts a considered diamond buyer and defends the price.

Quick wins

Live within a week

Low effort, no rebuild — mostly configuration and content.

i.

Switch on the reviews you already collect

Judge.me is installed but empty. Request reviews from recent customers and surface the widget on every product.

ii.

Add a ring sizing guide

A simple printable and at-home measure on every ring page — immediate reduction in hesitation and returns.

iii.

Bring certification onto the PDP

Show the four Cs and the IGI report on each diamond, alongside the UK hallmark.

iv.

Enable instalments

Activate an interest-free option at checkout so four-figure pieces reach more of your traffic.

v.

Point the consultation at a real appointment

Replace the generic form route with a bookable slot for virtual or in-person bespoke appointments.

vi.

Fix the Earrings link

Resolve it to a curated earrings collection rather than a generic page.
