Section 01

Executive Summary

You've built something genuinely special. The brand is warm, the photography is excellent, and the product range speaks for itself. What this audit is about is making sure the people who land on your site — most of them on a phone, most of them with a specific occasion in mind — find the quickest possible path from “I want this” to “it's ordered.”

The short version: right now, your site is working against a motivated buyer at the moments that matter most. There's no direct route to shop from the navigation. The Add to Cart button appears only after significant scrolling. And there's no delivery information on the page where the purchase decision is made. These aren't design problems — they're structural gaps, and every single one is fixable inside your existing Shopify theme in a matter of hours.

Section 03

Upsell & Cross-Sell Strategy

You're already sitting on the ingredients for a higher average order value. None of this requires new products — only placing existing products at the right moment in the journey.

Upsell: size and personalisation

Visual size buttons showing “Feeds X people” frame the decision around occasion fit, not price. The larger option feels like the right choice for the occasion rather than simply the more expensive one. Your add-ons (Plaque £5, Sign £1.50, Candles £3, Sprinkles £1) grouped as a “Make it special” panel rather than scattered checkboxes would materially lift attach rate.

Cross-sell: completing the occasion

A birthday cake buyer is a high-probability Banana Minis buyer (£28). A gluten-free cake buyer wants to see your full GF range. A first-time visitor hesitating on a full cake can be nudged to Banana Bites as a lower-commitment entry. These are not new products — they just need placing at the right moment.

AOV opportunity: If 15% of orders added one cross-sell (average £28) and 25% added one add-on (average £3.50 weighted), average order value increases by approximately £5–£8 per order without acquiring a single new customer.

Section 06

Quick Wins: This Week

Five changes you can make today in Shopify admin or the theme editor. No developer needed.

01

Add “Order Cakes” to the navigation

Shopify admin → Navigation → add /collections/all as the first header item. 2 minutes.

02

Rewrite the announcement bar

Single line: “Order by Sunday for Tuesday delivery · FIRSTBITE = 10% off → Order now.” Link to /discount/FIRSTBITE?redirect=/collections/all.

03

Reorder size variants

Drag the 8" Mama to first in Shopify product editor. Changes the opening price from £86 to £64 instantly across every affected PDP.

04

Install Hextom Sticky Add to Cart

Free on the Shopify App Store. Install and configure in 10 minutes. Resolves the buried-ATC problem across every product page sitewide immediately.

05

Add delivery copy to product descriptions

“Freshly baked to order — ready in 72 hours. Nationwide delivery Tuesday to Sunday.” One paragraph per product via the Shopify product editor.
