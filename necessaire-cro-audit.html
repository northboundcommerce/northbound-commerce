01 — Executive Summary

The foundations are strong.
The friction is fixable.

Nécessaire is a rare thing: a brand that has earned genuine category authority through product quality and considered design. The visual identity is clean, the formulas are differentiated, and the B Corp story is credible. What holds the site back is a conversion layer that hasn't kept pace with the catalogue's complexity — specifically, the variant matrix for formulas and scents creates decision paralysis that costs new customers before they reach checkout.

10

Conversion and UX friction points identified across homepage, navigation, PDP, and post-cart

4

Quick wins requiring no design system changes — implement within a single sprint

1

Structural change — formula guidance — with the highest single impact on new customer conversion

The headline opportunity: Nécessaire sells four formula variants of the same Body Wash (Multi-Oil, Multi-Mineral, Multi-Acid, Barrier Complex) across five scents. That's up to twenty permutations — with no on-page guidance to help a new visitor choose. Adding a simple "Best for:" line per formula chip and a skin type entry point would reduce bounce from the variant selector and measurably increase first purchase rate.

02 — Top 10 Findings

Prioritised by revenue impact.

# Finding Detail Impact Type
01 Formula selector: no guidance Four formula variants are presented as equal options with no "best for" copy, skin type mapping, or differentiation hierarchy. First-time visitors cannot self-select confidently, increasing bounce before add-to-cart. HighRevenue
02 Subscribe & Save invisible at decision moment The 15% Subscribe & Save offer sits in the header bar and on a separate page — absent from the PDP at the point of purchase. On-PDP subscription toggle is one of the highest-uplift CRO interventions for replenishable categories. HighRevenue
03 Scent selection: no sensory bridge Scent notes listed as ingredients (Eucalyptus, Siberian Fir) rather than as sensory experience. No mood or profile descriptor accompanies the scent picker. New visitors buying fragrance-forward body wash cannot make a confident choice without prior product knowledge. HighUX
04 Discovery set upsell absent from PDPs The Body Wash Discovery Collection exists but is not surfaced from within individual Body Wash PDPs. A first-time visitor who can't choose a scent has no obvious resolution — they either pick at random or abandon. MediumRevenue
05 Homepage hero chasing newness over revenue The hero leads with "The Detox Shampoo" — a new product with 7 reviews. The core body franchise (most reviews, largest repeat purchase base) is below the fold. The homepage hero should maximise conversion from arriving traffic, which skews toward body. MediumRevenue
06 Review helpful votes displaying zero counts "0 people voted yes / 0 people voted no" is rendered beneath every review. This signals an abandoned feature — it undermines trust in the review system. Either remove the helpful voting UI or suppress zero-count states. MediumTrust
07 "We Design The Necessary™" trust bar opaque The homepage trust bar uses Nécessaire's own branded terminology without explaining its meaning to a first-time visitor. In the same row, "We Are B Corp Certified" and "We Give 1% Back" communicate immediately — the brand line does not. MediumTrust
08 No visible loyalty mechanic beyond referral The only post-purchase retention mechanic visible on-site is "Give $10 Get $10" referral. Subscribe & Save exists but isn't prominently sold as a loyalty benefit. No points/rewards layer is visible for a brand with strong repeat purchase intent. MediumRevenue
09 "Objects" navigation label unreadable "Objects" is used as a category name for what appears to be accessories or tools. This is opaque to any visitor unfamiliar with the brand's taxonomy — it communicates nothing about what's inside the category. LowUX
10 Waitlist CTA under-leveraged Out-of-stock products display a waitlist form but without urgency signal, estimated restock date, or "meanwhile, try this" cross-sell. Visitors landing on an OOS PDP from paid or organic have high intent — a lateral recommendation preserves the acquisition cost. LowUX

03 — Revenue Opportunities

Where the money is left on the table.

Subscription uplift

On-PDP Subscribe & Save toggle

Body wash and lotion are high-frequency replenishment purchases. Brands in this category consistently see 20–35% of new customers select a subscription when presented directly at add-to-cart — with no promotional prompt required. The 15% saving is currently not shown at the point of decision.

→ Show subscribe toggle with price comparison beneath the ATC button

AOV uplift

Discovery set as scent resolution

The Body Wash Discovery Collection (all four scents, 100ml each) serves a natural "I can't choose" customer. It isn't surfaced at the scent picker on single-product PDPs. Adding a "Try all four scents" module at that moment converts uncertain visitors and adds $15–25 to AOV.

→ Surface discovery set inline when scent picker is engaged

LTV uplift

Post-purchase loyalty layer

Nécessaire has catalogue depth for a meaningful loyalty programme — body wash, lotion, treatment, deodorant, hair, lip, and hand. A points-based model encourages cross-category trial, the single highest predictor of long-term customer value in personal care DTC.

→ Evaluate Yotpo or Loyalty Lion integration alongside Subscribe & Save

Funnel capture

Waitlist → cross-sell redirect

Out-of-stock PDPs dead-end with a waitlist form. A "While you wait" module showing in-stock alternatives in the same formula or scent family keeps high-intent visitors in the purchase funnel rather than exiting to compare or abandon.

→ Add cross-sell rail to waitlist confirmation state on all OOS PDPs

①

Formula chips with skin type guidance

Each formula variant carries a one-line skin type descriptor beneath the name. Reduces decision paralysis for new visitors and eliminates the "which one is for me?" support ticket. One line of copy per variant, applied via metafield.

②

Sticky ATC bar — mobile and desktop

The sticky add-to-cart bar persists as the visitor scrolls down the extended PDP. On mobile, it anchors to the bottom of the viewport showing product name, price, and one-tap add. On desktop, it spans the full width below the product hero. Both update dynamically with variant selection.

③

Awards bar — scrolling ticker from live awards page

The five most recent awards (2025–2026) run as an auto-scrolling ticker immediately below the PDP hero. Links directly to the awards page for full validation. Dynamically updatable via Shopify metaobjects — no hardcoded content.

④

Trust signal bar (homepage signals, moved to PDP)

B Corp, 1% For Planet, Plastic Neutral, and EU Compliant are shown on the homepage but not on PDPs. Moving them below the PDP hero reinforces purchase confidence at the moment of conversion, not only on brand discovery pages.

⑤

Sparks UGC video carousel

The Spark app imports TikTok and Instagram video content, tags products, and renders shoppable video carousels with zero page speed impact. Surfaces authentic usage context and social proof at the point of purchase decision — a high-value trust signal for beauty and body care categories.

⑥

Customer reviews without zero-count voting

Reviews rendered without the "0 people voted yes / 0 people voted no" helpful vote counts. Third review naturally surfaces the formula selector friction as a customer pain point — useful qualitative validation of finding #01.

⑦

Footprint brand story on PDP

The Footprint page exists as a standalone destination but is invisible from the PDP. Moving it inline — in a dark, editorial treatment — reinforces the brand's sustainability credentials at the exact moment a new visitor is deciding whether to trust the brand. Particularly high-value for first purchase conversion.

06 — Quick Wins

Ship within a sprint.

Revenue

Add Subscribe & Save toggle to all PDPs

Surface the existing subscription offer inline at add-to-cart. No new programme required — just expose the feature at the right moment.

UX

Add "Best for:" line to each formula chip

One line of copy per formula mapped to skin type. Copy only — applied via metafield or theme snippet. No design changes.

UX

Add sensory descriptors to scent picker

One-line scent character description per variant, rendering beneath the selector on selection. Copy update via product metafields.

Trust

Suppress zero-count review voting UI

Hide "0 people voted yes / 0 people voted no" states via CSS or review platform settings. Removes a visible signal of abandonment.

UX

Rename "Objects" to "Accessories" in nav

A single theme translation update. No design, no development. Immediately improves navigation comprehension for new visitors.

Trust

Replace "We Design The Necessary™" trust bar copy

Replace with a customer-benefit statement — e.g. "We formulate without the EU's 1,300 banned substances". Same visual slot; significantly higher clarity for first-time visitors.

07 — 90-Day Roadmap

A sequenced plan, not a wishlist.

Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2

Copy and configuration quick wins

Deploy all six quick wins: subscribe toggle, formula guidance copy, scent descriptors, review voting suppression, nav rename, and trust bar copy update. Establish a baseline for all PDP conversion metrics before any structural work begins.

Low effortHigh impact

Phase 2 — Weeks 3–6

PDP structural improvements + Sparks + sticky ATC

Implement the full PDP layout: formula chip grid with skin type guidance, scent descriptor metafields, Discovery Set inline module, Subscribe & Save toggle, sticky ATC bar, awards ticker, trust signal bar, Sparks UGC carousel, and the Footprint brand story section. A/B test new PDP against current — primary metric: add-to-cart rate from new visitors.

Medium effortHigh impact

Phase 3 — Weeks 7–9

Navigation and collection pages

Roll out the mega-menu with formula and scent columns. Add a "By Skin Concern" entry point and build a corresponding filtered collection page. Evaluate a skin type quiz as a collection page gateway module — high impact on new visitor acquisition from SEO and paid social.

Medium effortMedium impact

Phase 4 — Weeks 10–12

LTV infrastructure

Evaluate loyalty programme integration (Yotpo Loyalty or Loyalty Lion) alongside the existing Subscribe & Save programme. Build a "While you wait" cross-sell module for out-of-stock PDPs. Review post-purchase email flows for Subscribe & Save upsell moments.

High effortRevenue — LTV
