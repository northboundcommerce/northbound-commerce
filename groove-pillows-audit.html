Executive Summary

Groove Pillows has built an enviable DTC brand: 12,780+ reviews, a clear pain-relief positioning, a "Best Pillow 2025" claim, and a tight product range at an accessible price point. The foundations are strong. However, the PDP — the single most important page on the site — leaves conversion on the table through a cluttered above-the-fold hierarchy, buried trust signals, no structured product education, and a missing cross-sell/bundle strategy. This audit identifies ten high-impact opportunities and provides a full PDP mockup showing how Groove can close the gap between brand strength and commercial performance.

Section 01

Above the Fold & First Impression

High Impact

The H1 is duplicated and diluted. Two competing H1s create visual noise and split the focal point. A single, benefit-led H1 with a supporting subtitle would give the hero far more clarity.

High Impact

Price and ATC sit below the carousel on mobile. On narrower viewports the buying module falls beneath ten+ gallery images. Sticky ATC on mobile is essential.

Medium Impact

No Klarna / Clearpay messaging at ATF. Instalments messaging ("4 × £8.75") is absent entirely. Even at £35, BNPL copy lifts perceived affordability and nudges multi-unit purchases.

Section 02

Conversion Rate Optimisation

High Impact

No structured cross-sell or bundle module at ATF. "Bundle & Save" exists but sits far below fold as a text link. A visual bundle builder would drive AOV immediately.

Medium Impact

Generic "Add to Cart" copy. A value-reinforced CTA — "Add to Cart — 100 Night Trial" — would lower perceived risk at the point of commitment.

Medium Impact

No urgency or scarcity messaging. No stock indicators, no delivery countdown. Subtle urgency nudges improve checkout click-through.

Section 03

Brand & Positioning

High Impact

"850+ Medical Practitioners" is buried. The single most powerful conversion asset is rendered as a small icon strip. It should feature prominently in the buy box.

Lower Impact

Good Housekeeping badge has no context. It appears as a gallery image. Shoppers who don't swipe that far never see it.

Section 04

UX & UI Design

High Impact

Gallery is heavy on testimonials, light on education. Several gallery images are overlaid review quotes — diluting both product showcase and social proof. A cleaner gallery with "How It Works" diagrams would convert better.

Medium Impact

No sleep-position selector. A "How do you sleep?" selector revealing tailored copy would personalise the single-SKU PDP and address the #1 pre-purchase objection.

Section 05

Content & Storytelling

Medium Impact

No "How It Works" section. The product has a compelling design story but the page relies on images alone. A structured feature section would educate and convert simultaneously.

Lower Impact

FAQ not surfaced early. Common objections should be addressed within the buy box via an accordion.

Section 06

Technical & SEO

Medium Impact

Missing descriptive alt text. Multiple Sanity CMS images use generic or empty alt attributes — a missed opportunity for image-search traffic and accessibility.

Lower Impact

No visible breadcrumbs. A simple trail would improve orientation and search snippets.

Strategic Experiments

Longer-Horizon Tests

Sleep-Position Personalisation: Three-option selector that swaps hero image and feature copy. Sells one product to three audiences without additional SKUs.

Bundle Builder: Visual module — Pillow + Pillowcase + Eye Mask at combined price with savings callout. 15–25% AOV uplift typical.

Design Rationale

Gallery

Reordered: 1) Hero product shot, 2) Dimensions overlay, 3) "How the Groove works" diagram, 4) Materials close-up, 5) Lifestyle. Testimonial images removed from gallery — handled in dedicated below-fold section.

Medical Kicker

"Recommended by 850+ Medical Practitioners" moved from buried trust bar into kicker position above the H1. Establishes authority before the customer reads anything else.

Klarna

Instalment messaging added beneath price — currently absent from the live PDP. Even at £35, BNPL copy improves perceived affordability and encourages multi-unit / bundle purchases.

Sleep Selector

New interactive element. Tapping reveals tailored copy — e.g. "Side sleepers: the groove cradles your neck while raised edges support spinal alignment." Personalises a single-SKU PDP and addresses the #1 pre-purchase objection.

CTA Copy

Button now carries value reinforcement: "100 Night Trial" addresses risk at the point of commitment. The live CTA is generic "ADD TO CART".

Below Fold

Supporting content — product science, video, expert stats, reviews, cross-sell — arranged as quiet editorial sections with labelled dividers. Each section breathes; none competes with the buy box above.

Cross-Sell

Visual module replaces the current buried "Bundle & Save" text link. Pillow + Pillowcase + Bundle at a glance, with outlined CTAs that don't overpower the primary purchase.
