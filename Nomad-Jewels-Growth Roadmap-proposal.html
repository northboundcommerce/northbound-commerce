The question you raised

What role should pricing play in the experience?

We don't see this as a decision about whether prices should be visible or hidden. We see it as a decision about how pricing supports the experience you want to create.

For a brand building a luxury lifestyle platform, pricing is another touchpoint in the customer journey. It should create confidence where transparency is needed, and exclusivity where personal guidance adds real value.

Our recommendation is a segmented model.

Display a clear price, or a confident "From", on signature and entry collections, to build trust and reduce unnecessary friction for digitally native luxury buyers. Reserve "Price on Request" and private consultations for bespoke, made-to-order and high jewellery, where the value lies as much in the conversation as in the piece itself.

The success of this approach depends entirely on the experience behind it. If requesting a price leads to a generic contact form and a delayed response, the journey feels like an obstacle. If it opens the door to a personalised consultation, thoughtful recommendations and a concierge-level experience, it reinforces the positioning you are building.

Today, "Price on Request" is already the default on the Ring Infinity product page. The opportunity isn't simply to keep it or remove it; it's to make it a deliberate part of a customer journey that earns the right to ask for a conversation.

This shouldn't be settled by opinion. It should be designed, measured and refined. As part of the Growth Roadmap, we'd define where each pricing model is most appropriate, map the journeys that support it, and establish a measurement framework to evaluate not only conversion, enquiry quality and average order value, but also customer lifetime value.

That final measure matters most. Nomad Jewels' ambition isn't simply to sell more jewellery; it's to build enduring relationships through content, community, experiences and craftsmanship. The right pricing strategy should be judged not only by how many conversations it starts, but by whether it attracts the customers who become your most engaged advocates: those who return to purchase again, attend your events, and grow with the brand over time.
