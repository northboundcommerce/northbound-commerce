Our approach

A project structured to manage risk, control cost, and deliver usable output at every phase.

Each phase has a defined scope, fixed cost, and a clear sign-off point. This means Fairview retains full control of the project investment at every stage — no phase begins until the previous one is approved and paid.

Phases 3–6 follow a design-then-build workflow: no development begins until the corresponding design templates have been reviewed and signed off. This prevents expensive rework and keeps the project predictable.

On the API integrations: Nausys (worldwide charter) and Rightboat (brokerage) are the most technically complex elements of this build. We recommend allocating adequate discovery time in Phase 1 to review both APIs before committing to Phase 5 costs. Any undocumented complexity discovered at that stage will be flagged before development begins.
On translations: WPML is included in Phase 6. The cost covers technical setup and the translation workflow infrastructure. Content translation itself (RU, FR, ZH) is excluded — see the exclusions section.

Scope boundaries

What's not included

—
Content writing
All page copy, yacht descriptions, destination narratives, and blog articles. Northbound can manage a copywriting brief and quote separately.
—
Content translation (RU / FR / ZH)
WPML infrastructure is included in Phase 6. Translation of all content into Russian, French, and Chinese is excluded and should be commissioned separately. Note: Chinese and Russian markets require dedicated content strategy, not auto-translation.
—
Photography & videography
All photography, video production, and post-production. The art direction guidelines produced in the audit are provided to guide commissioning.
—
Third-party software licences
Annual costs for: WPML (~£99), ACF Pro (~£49), WP Rocket (~£49), Yoast Premium (~£99), Gravity Forms (~£159), Ultimate Member Pro (~£149 if portal in scope). Total ~£604/year.
—
API subscription costs
Nausys API and Rightboat API subscription/access fees charged by those platforms directly. These will be confirmed during Phase 1 discovery.
—
CRM platform (HubSpot / Pipedrive)
CRM platform licensing. Gravity Forms integration to connect enquiry forms to the chosen CRM is included in the build. CRM setup and configuration is excluded.
—
Ongoing hosting
WordPress hosting on Kinsta or WP Engine. Recommended: Kinsta Business (~£130–200/month) for Singapore CDN node. Cloudflare free tier is included in the build scope.
—
Post-launch changes outside retainer
Any development work requested after the 5-day post-launch support window will be quoted and billed at our standard day rate of £875 + VAT.
Terms & conditions

Key terms

Revisions
2 rounds of revisions are included per phase. Additional revision rounds are billed at £875/day + VAT. Revision rounds do not carry over between phases.
Client delays
Timelines assume prompt feedback within 5 business days of each milestone delivery. Delays exceeding 10 business days may result in timeline extension and rebooking. Northbound reserves the right to charge a holding fee of £500/week for delays beyond 15 business days.
Intellectual property
All design and development work is transferred to Fairview Yachting in full upon receipt of final payment. Northbound Commerce retains the right to reference the project in case studies and portfolio materials.
Scope changes
Material changes to project scope (new features, additional pages, integration changes) will be quoted as a change request before work begins. Minor scope additions of under 4 hours are absorbed where reasonable.
API dependency
Phase 5 pricing is contingent on standard API access and documentation. Should Nausys or Rightboat API access involve non-standard authentication, rate limiting, or missing endpoints, a revised quote will be issued before Phase 5 commences.
Proposal validity
This proposal is valid for 60 days from the date of issue. Northbound Commerce reserves the right to requote if the project start date is more than 90 days after proposal acceptance.
