REFY Beauty — Ecommerce Growth Audit

Site: refybeauty.com · Platform: Shopify · Category: Premium DTC beauty

Executive Summary

REFY has built a category-defining brand on the back of a clean visual identity, founder credibility (Jess Hunt) and hero SKUs like Brow Sculpt. The site is considered and on-brand — the opportunity is in turning that brand strength into more conversion and AOV. The homepage could do more to tell the brand story, the hero currently leans on a single product launch, and existing social proof is underused — strong review counts and retailer credibility exist but aren't surfaced above the fold. The promotional stack (free Concealer, 15% member offer, free shipping) is strong but would benefit from tighter orchestration. The biggest unlocks: a sharper hero, smarter bundling and cross-sell, and a quiz-led shade-finder funnel.

1. Above-the-Fold & First Impression

Value Proposition

The hero leads with "Introducing REFY Skin Base" — a product launch rather than a brand promise. There's an opportunity for first-time visitors to immediately understand what REFY is, who it's for, and what makes it different. The "Simplifying Beauty" tagline lives in the footer where it can't do that work.

Visual Hierarchy

Clean and considered. A secondary anchor or content module after the hero would give the eye somewhere to go and increase the chance of engagement before the fold drops off.

CTA Strength

"Shop Now" is functional but neutral. Adding a price anchor, proof point or specific product reference would lift click-through. The free Concealer threshold (£50) and free shipping (£30) are also worth surfacing more prominently than the rotating top bar allows.

2. Conversion Optimisation

Friction Points

  • The country selector modal on entry adds a step before the user sees product. A silent geo-detect with a dismissible banner would reduce that initial friction.
  • Three competing offers in the announcement bar (£30 free shipping, 15% sign-up, free Concealer £50+) compete for attention. Leading with one — likely the £50 free gift, since it drives AOV — would let each offer work harder.
  • Free Concealer is shown in the cart drawer at £0 — nicely done — but the unlock progress isn't visualised ("£X away from your free Concealer") which is a meaningful AOV lever.

Trust Signals

Review counts are genuinely impressive (Brow Sculpt 4,060 reviews / 4.9★) but quietly placed. A homepage trust bar (press, retailer logos, aggregate review count, "Loved by 500k+") would put them to work. Sephora and Space NK stockist credibility is also worth surfacing.

Product Listing Effectiveness

Tile design is strong: hero shot + on-model swap, shade count, price, rating. Add-to-cart from tile works well for single-shade SKUs; for multi-shade products a quick-shade overlay would remove a click on your highest-converting items.

Cart/Checkout

The standard Shopify checkout is assumed solid. The cart drawer is on-brand and could be extended with: a progress bar to next reward, a low-cost cross-sell module ("Complete the look — Lip Gloss £16"), and a clearer returns/shipping reassurance line.

3. Brand & Positioning

Differentiation

REFY's real moat is founder-led, multi-functional, brow-first heritage. There's an opportunity to tell that story more visibly on-site. Without it, REFY can read similarly to other "clean minimal beauty" brands (Merit, Saie, Kosas) on visuals alone.

Messaging Clarity

"Simplify Beauty" is a strong line that deserves more prominence than the bottom of the page. Pairing it with a concrete proof point — e.g. "Multi-functional products. Half the routine." — would sharpen it further.

Audience Alignment

Visuals and shade range read inclusive and millennial/Gen-Z premium — well-targeted. A segmented entry path (e.g. "New to REFY?" vs "Shop the routine") would help first-timers and loyalists into the right funnel.

4. UX / UI

Navigation & IA

The mega-menu is well-organised by category with strong visual merchandising. Two opportunities: "REFY World" (loyalty?) is opaque to new users and could carry more value if its purpose was clearer, and Shade Finder sits inside Face — promoting it to primary nav would put one of the highest-intent conversion tools in front of more visitors.

Mobile Experience

Likely strong given Shopify Dawn-based theme and mobile hero asset. Confirm: sticky ATC on PDPs, thumb-reachable shade swatches, and that the rotating announcement bar doesn't eat 60px of screen real estate.

Page Speed Heuristics

Heavy use of Speedsize CDN suggests image performance is taken seriously. Risk areas: video tiles in "Community Favourites", Okendo widget weight on PDPs, and modal scripts firing on first paint.

5. Content & Copy

Headline Strength

Product subtitles are excellent — "12-hour hold mascara that sculpts, lifts and lengthens" is benefit-led, specific, ownable. The homepage hero copy ("This is your skin, your way") has room to match that bar.

Product Storytelling

Strong on the "what" (function, hold, finish), with room to develop the "why" (formulation philosophy, ingredient story, founder POV). At a £18-£24 price point, more story would help justify the premium against drugstore alternatives.

Social Proof

A real opportunity area. With 14,000+ reviews across hero SKUs and a co-founder with significant personal reach, a homepage UGC grid, editorial press strip or "as worn by" celebrity callouts would put assets you already have to work.

6. SEO (Light)

On-Page

Title tag is descriptive and keyword-rich ("Vegan & Cruelty Free Makeup & Skincare"). The homepage H1 appears to be the logo wordmark — a real H1 like "Multi-functional makeup & skincare. Less routine, more results." would do more for both clarity and SEO.

Content Opportunities

  • Comparison content: "REFY vs Merit", "Best brow gel for thin brows" — high-intent, low competition for the brand.
  • Shade-matching content: long-tail wins around "skin tint for [skin type]" with the quiz as conversion endpoint.
  • Tutorial-led blog content tied to PDPs (currently the blog is hidden under "More").

Quick Wins (Next 30 Days)

  • Hero copy rewrite — lead with brand promise, not Skin Base launch. Move Skin Base to a secondary hero slot.
  • Cart progress bar to the £50 free-Concealer threshold; expect 3–6% AOV lift.
  • Trust bar under hero: "Loved by 500,000+ · 4.9★ across 14,000 reviews · Stocked at Sephora & Space NK".
  • Single announcement at a time — kill the rotation, A/B test which offer drives most revenue per session.
  • Sticky ATC on mobile PDPs if not already live.
  • H1 rewrite on homepage for SEO + clarity.

Strategic Experiments

  • Routine quiz funnel — combine Shade Finder with a "Build your REFY routine" quiz that outputs a personalised bundle at a small discount. Quizzes typically drive 2–3× site CVR for first-time buyers.
  • Subscription / replenishment for hero SKUs (Brow Sculpt, Lash Sculpt, Lip Sculpt) — recurring revenue lever currently absent.
  • "REFY World" repositioning as a clear loyalty programme with visible tiers, points and member-only drops. Currently it reads as a brand world, with room to add more direct value exchange.
  • Founder-led PDP modules — short Jess Hunt video on each hero PDP explaining the "why". Leverages your single biggest marketing asset.
  • Bundle architecture overhaul — current Signature Sculpt at £120 (save £40) is the only meaningful bundle. Build 3 tiered routines (£60 / £100 / £160) by use case (Everyday / Evening / Full Face).
